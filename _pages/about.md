I am a postdoc at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign studying neutron stars, extreme gravity, and nuclear astrophysics.  My interests lie at the broad intersections of gravity which is strong enough to hold together dense nuclear matter, and the extreme nuclear interactions which prevent this matter from collapsing under its own self gravity.  

See my [Github](https://github.com/isaaclegred) for more detailed project information.
