I am a postdoc at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign studying neutron stars, extreme gravity, and nuclear astrophysics. My interests lie at the intersection of gravity strong enough to hold together dense nuclear matter and the extreme nuclear interactions that keep this matter from collapsing under its own gravity. I approach these questions by combining Bayesian inference on astrophysical data with nuclear and gravitational theory and numerical simulation. I received my Ph.D. from Caltech in 2025, advised by Katerina Chatziioannou ([thesis](/publication/2025-05-23-phd-legred)).

See my [GitHub](https://github.com/isaaclegred) for code and more detailed project information.

Research
======

**The dense-matter equation of state.** Neutron-star masses, radii, and tidal deformabilities, measured with gravitational waves, X-ray pulsar timing, and radio pulsars, tell us how matter behaves above nuclear saturation density. I build [nonparametric](/publication/2022-03-29-implicit-correlations-eos) and [semiparametric](/publication/2025-07-03-inferring-eos-semiparametric) models of the equation of state, so that these constraints are driven by the data rather than by modeling assumptions. I have used them to measure the impact of [new pulsar observations](/publication/2021-09-01-Impact-of-the-PSR-mathrmJ07406620-radius-constraint-on-the-properties-of-high-density-matter), to search for [phase transitions](/publication/2023-05-12-phase-transition-phenomenology), and to carry out [unified inference from the neutron-star crust up to perturbative-QCD densities](/publication/2025-05-19-unified-nonparametric-eos).

**Connecting astrophysics and nuclear theory.** Astrophysical constraints are most useful when they can be turned back into statements about nuclear physics. I study [how astrophysical and nuclear information combine](/publication/2025-01-10-interplay-astrophysics-nuclear), [where relativistic mean-field theory breaks down](/publication/2025-05-12-nonparametric-extensions-rmf) at high density, and how [neural-network emulators make Bayesian calibration of chiral mean-field models practical](/publication/2026-07-14-neural-calibration-chiral-mean-field).

**Neutron-star structure and universality.** Why do sequences of stars reach a maximum mass and then turn unstable? Why do some neutron-star properties follow relations that barely depend on the equation of state? I study these questions both with [flexible equation-of-state models](/publication/2024-02-12-assessing-eos-independent-relations) and with [fixed-point descriptions of stellar structure](/publication/2026-03-27-why-stellar-sequences-turn-over).

**Gravitational-wave astrophysics.** I use gravitational-wave observations to learn about compact objects and where they come from: [whether a low-mass merger contains a neutron star or a black hole](/publication/2024-03-12-using-eos-classify-mergers), [what produces the peak near 10 solar masses in the black-hole mass distribution](/publication/2026-04-01-failed-supernovae-10msun-peak), and [how dynamical tides drive the orbital evolution of a neutron star paired with a much lighter primordial black hole](/publication/2026-10-23-ns-pbh-dynamical-tides). I also contribute to equation-of-state and population analyses in the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA Collaboration.

**Simulating neutron stars.** Modeling neutron-star mergers requires numerical relativity. As part of the SXS collaboration, I have developed [equation-of-state](/publication/2023-01-31-simulating-eos-spectre) and hydrodynamics infrastructure for the [SpECTRE](https://spectre-code.org) code, and I have studied [premerger emission from crustal oscillations](/publication/2024-07-24-alfven-wave-dynamics).
